# Analyze

After delivering assessments and sharing results, use the **Analyze** phase of Getting Started to explore patterns and follow outcomes. Select a card for its detailed in-product guide and use the action button to open the workspace or choose the account/assessment to investigate.

| Card | Next task |
| --- | --- |
| [Explore your dashboard](../dashboard/index.md) | Compare accounts on one assessment type, configure components, export data, and open Analytics. Directors can inspect program activity and impact. |
| [Explore the Client Portal](../client-portal/ae-notes.md) | Choose an account, use View as Client, and check the client Library and enabled reports. Preview retains staff permissions. |
| [Understand your ecosystem](../ecosystem/index.md) | Map locations, scores, and recorded impact, then filter by geography, industry, or account characteristics. |
| [Track and measure impact](../impact-tracker/index.md) | Maintain the actions reviewed at first closure, add project links, and record confirmed or estimated outcomes. |

Assessment scores, report findings, action status, and recorded impact answer different questions. Check the selected type, timeframe, filters, and aggregation before comparing results. Keep missing data distinct from zero.
