# Permissions and User Roles

Available pages and actions depend on your role, account access, and your organization’s enabled features. Getting Started uses the same role-specific workflow so clients and staff see relevant guidance.

## Staff roles

**Administrators** manage organization configuration and the administrative tools enabled for the tenant, such as branding, email templates, users, intake forms, and report templates.

**Account Executives** work with the accounts and assessments available to them: prepare assessments, invite respondents, review results, build reports, and follow up on actions. Library tools may be available where assessment editing is enabled; administration and publishing actions follow their own permissions.

**Directors access** enables the program-wide dashboard view. It does not mean the viewer should interpret a program total as an individual assessment’s result.

## Client role

Clients work within their own account. They can complete assigned assessments, start an assessment from a client-visible Library template, and review the results and reports made available to them. Where the newer portal features are enabled, clients can maintain their account information and follow up through Actions.

Clients use the supplied templates; they do not manage the staff Library or tenant settings. Account information, user controls, and editing actions follow the permissions offered in their portal.

## Client privacy

For a self-created assessment, **Privacy → Hide from account executives & admins** keeps the assessment within the account team. Ordinary administrators and AEs cannot view that private work. **View as Client** retains staff permissions and does not bypass this restriction.

Template client visibility is a separate control: it determines which eligible published assessment types appear in the client Library. Report availability is also separate and can be managed for individual assessments through **Account → Reports**.

## Resolve missing access

Check the current account, role, assessment status, date window, and enabled feature before expecting a button or page. Ask your administrator about missing staff tools; ask your account executive about assigned assessments and report access. Use the invitation and role controls in [Manage Users](users.md) rather than assuming a contact entry alone grants access.
